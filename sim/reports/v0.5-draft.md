# Economy simulation report: v0.5

Rules: preset `v0.5`. 300 matches per setting, each on its own shuffled map, seats drawn from the six scripted bots. Rules are docs/design/theme-and-board.md with the gap-review edits applied. Every match was reconciled phase by phase (coins and goods) and no bot submitted an invalid action unless noted. Results are relative to these bots: they are plausible players, not optimal ones, and the searching bot in section 3 is the check on what they miss.
