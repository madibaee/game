# Economy simulation report: v0.5-short

Rules: preset `v0.5-short`. 300 matches per setting, each on its own shuffled map, seats drawn from the six scripted bots. Every match was reconciled phase by phase (coins and goods) and no bot submitted an invalid action unless noted. Results are relative to these bots: they are plausible players, not optimal ones, and the searching bot in section 3 is the check on what they miss.
